# AUT-01 — Apply cost delegation

Version: 1.2
Effective: 2026-08-01 through 2026-12-31
Owner: Finance / supply chain
Scope: BIC Retail Studio demonstration policy set.

Every cost needs human approval: up to EUR 500 Operations; above EUR 500 Supply chain manager; above EUR 1500 Supply chain director and Finance. Unknown costs must be quoted first.

Evaluation: deterministic controls use this version. Retrieval explains the rule; it does not authorize an exception.
