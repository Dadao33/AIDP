# SLA-01 — Protect service dates

Version: 1.2
Effective: 2026-08-01 through 2026-12-31
Owner: Customer service
Scope: BIC Retail Studio demonstration policy set.

Arrive before the relevant demand date. Separate the firm customer promise from forecast demand. A residual firm shortfall requires commercial review.

Evaluation: deterministic controls use this version. Retrieval explains the rule; it does not authorize an exception.
